02 — Process

Here’s how a project goes.

You’ll always know what’s being built, what it costs, and when it ships.

1

I learn your operation

We walk through your workflow, your data, and where things get stuck. Then I tell you exactly what the system needs to do.

2

You see it early

I show you working versions from the start. Your feedback goes straight to me, the person writing the code.

3

We go live

I launch it, move your data over, and show your team how to use it. Your business keeps running the whole time.

4

I stay on it

After launch I monitor, fix, and improve. As you grow, the system grows with you.
